# Sleepy Hollow Cast Repository

Status: LOCKED CHARACTER BUILD v1.0
Canonical use: illustration, audio, comic, animation/film, cover, and other media.
Core rule: stable identity + changing scene state.
Verification gate: **Is this still unmistakably the same character?**

## Main cast

### Ichabod Crane
**Identity lock**
- Very tall and exceedingly lank
- Narrow shoulders, long arms and legs
- Large dangling hands, very large feet
- Small head, flat at top, huge ears
- Large green/glassy eyes
- Long narrow snipe nose
- Scant forehead
- Rusty black suit for formal scenes
- Small wool hat on the Van Tassel ride
- Instantly crane-like silhouette

**Core**
- Schoolmaster and singing-master
- Educated relative to rural community
- Vain about singing and dancing
- Huge appetite
- Ambitious and socially ingratiating
- Credulous about witchcraft, ghosts, omens and local legend
- Imaginative and easily frightened at night
- Persistent but avoids direct physical confrontation
- Comic without being stupid

**Scene-variable**
Expression, posture, gesture, lighting, mood and action may change.
Identity, body proportions and recognizable silhouette do not.

### Katrina Van Tassel
**Identity lock**
- About eighteen
- Plump, healthy, youthful, rosy-cheeked
- Warm prosperous rural beauty
- Blend of old and newer period fashion
- Gold family ornaments
- Old-fashioned stomacher
- Recognizable graceful silhouette

**Core**
- Socially confident
- A little coquettish
- Aware of attention
- Exercises choice and social leverage
- Warm but ambiguous in courtship

### Abraham “Brom Bones” Van Brunt
**Identity lock**
- Burly, broad-shouldered, physically powerful
- Short curly black hair
- Bluff but not unpleasant face
- Expression mixes fun and arrogance
- Strong athletic silhouette
- Cold-weather signature: fur cap with fox tail

**Core**
- Excellent horseman
- Competitive, boisterous, social, admired
- Ready for fight or frolic
- Mischief outweighs malice
- Rough good humor
- Theatrical prankster
- Jealous when romantically threatened

**Important scene-state rule**
At the dance, Brom is specifically jealous and brooding alone in a corner. This is a temporary scene state, not his permanent character.

### Baltus (“Balt”) Van Tassel
- Prosperous Dutch farmer
- Katrina’s father
- Round, jolly, contented face
- Hospitable, easy, indulgent father
- Open gestures, hearty laugh, relaxed smoker

### Mrs. Van Tassel
- Older Dutch-American farm wife
- Busy, practical, capable
- Household and poultry manager
- Clearly distinct from Katrina
- Not decorative background

### Hans Van Ripper
- Older Dutch farmer
- Choleric temperament
- Practical, property-conscious
- Owner of Gunpowder
- Harder-edged than Baltus

### Headless Horseman / Galloping Hessian
**Identity lock**
- Large mounted figure
- No visible head on shoulders
- Cloaked/muffled silhouette
- Apparent “head” carried at pommel during chase
- Strong consistent mounted silhouette
- Preserve ambiguity: terrifyingly real to Ichabod, never conclusively proven supernatural

**Core function**
Silence, pursuit, scale, inevitability, ambiguity, visual terror.

Do not turn into a talking fantasy villain or resolve the Brom/ghost ambiguity.

## Recurring animal cast

### Gunpowder
- Hans Van Ripper’s old plow horse
- Old, broken-down, gaunt, shaggy
- Ewe neck
- Hammer-like head
- Rusty burred mane and tail
- One damaged spectral eye
- Other eye retains fierce/devilish gleam
- Wayward, balks, plunges sideways, snorts
- Must remain recognizable standing, walking, fleeing, or returning riderless

### Daredevil
- Brom’s favorite horse
- Energetic, powerful, mischievous
- Difficult for anyone except Brom
- More athletic and spirited than Gunpowder
- Must remain consistent whenever shown

### Horseman’s black horse
- Black, powerful frame
- Imposing silhouette
- Larger/more threatening than Gunpowder
- Strong recurring identity
- Must not casually become Daredevil unless ambiguity is deliberately explored without being resolved

### Brom’s prank dog
- Distinctive recurring dog if illustrated
- Mischievous rather than monstrous
- Trained to whine ludicrously as mock rival singing pupil

## Ensemble rules
Supporting characters must never be cloned.

### Pupils
Vary age, build, hair, nose, expression, clothing wear and posture.

### Country damsels
Must not become variants of Katrina.

### Country swains
Must not become variants of Brom.

### Old Dutch wives / storytellers
Vary age, headwear, face shape, posture, hands and expression.

### Brom’s companions
Shared energy, not shared face.

### Van Tassel guests
Use genuine variety in age, build, dress, face and posture.

## Character constancy protocol

### Identity check
1. Is the face still the same person?
2. Are body proportions consistent?
3. Is the silhouette recognizable?
4. Are recurring clothing/prop cues consistent?
5. If mounted, is the correct horse present?

### Scene-state check
1. What is the character feeling now?
2. What posture follows from that?
3. What expression follows from that?
4. Is the change mood/action rather than identity drift?

### Evidence labels
- TEXT-LOCKED — directly supported by Irving
- PRODUCTION-LOCKED — approved design choice for consistency
- SCENE-VARIABLE — allowed to change with mood/action
- UNKNOWN — do not invent as canon

## Cross-media rule
The same cast repository drives:
- illustration
- audio
- comic
- animation/film
- cover
- promotional art

Presentation changes; character identity does not.

## Sleepy Hollow woodcut house style
- Antique black ink on warm cream paper
- Thin carved lines
- No heavy contour around every shape
- Main subject gets the labor; secondary areas remain quieter
- Open paper is allowed
- Apply Second-Proof Woodcut Reduction Rule
- Preserve believable period imperfection
- Supporting faces must be distinct
- Main verification question: **Would a real wood cutter / wood engraver do this?**
