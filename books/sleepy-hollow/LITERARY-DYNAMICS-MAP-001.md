## MAJOR CURVES

### Mood curve
TRANQUIL
→ DREAMY
→ COMIC
→ APPETITIVE
→ COMPETITIVE
→ FESTIVE
→ UNEASY
→ LONELY
→ OMINOUS
→ TERROR
→ DRY AFTERMATH
→ AMBIGUOUS COMIC RELEASE

### Pace curve
Very slow opening
→ moderate character/social build
→ slow feast
→ moderate party movement
→ gradual night tightening
→ rapid chase
→ sharp decompression
→ slow comic coda

### Tension curve
Low for most of first half
→ rivalry creates social pressure
→ ghost stories preload fear
→ rejection isolates Ichabod
→ night ride rises in steps
→ Horseman/chase = peak
→ aftermath collapses tension

### Comic curve
Strong through characterization and social life
→ temporarily suppressed during final ride
→ returns immediately through pumpkin/evidence/community explanation
→ peaks again in postscript

### Supernatural-pressure curve
Place/legend establishes background pressure
→ mostly recedes during social comedy
→ reloaded at fireside stories
→ becomes subjectively dominant in chase
→ deliberately weakened by aftermath evidence
→ remains culturally alive in final legend

## KEY TRANSITION JOINTS

These are especially important for later illustration and page-turn logic.

### T1 — S04 → S05
**Place mythology → comic protagonist**
Function: release supernatural pressure and make room for character comedy.

### T2 — S08 → S09
**Fearful imagination → romantic desire**
Function: redirect the story's engine without abandoning the flaw that will later matter.

### T3 — S12 → S13
**Distributed rivalry → convergence event**
Function: gather plot lines into the Van Tassel party.

### T4 — S16 → S17
**Dance/social triumph → ghost stories**
Function: the crucial warm-to-dark tonal hinge.

### T5 — S17 → S18
**Supernatural priming → romantic disappointment**
Function: combine freshly loaded fear with emotional vulnerability.

### T6 — S18 → S19
**Social world → solitary night road**
Function: remove witnesses, warmth, and comic safety.

### T7 — S22 → S23
**Uncertain rider → headless reveal**
Function: move from ambiguity to Ichabod's perceived certainty.

### T8 — S25 → S26
**Maximum motion/terror → still evidence**
Function: hard decompression and reinterpretation.

### T9 — S27 → S28
**Community supernatural verdict → rational alternative**
Function: reopen ambiguity.

### T10 — S29 → S30
**Legendary closure → storyteller undercut**
Function: stop the reader from treating the preceding legend as simple factual history.

## DOWNSTREAM PRODUCTION CONSEQUENCES

### Illustration placement
Illustrations should be considered only after checking:
- whether an image strengthens or interrupts the current pace;
- whether the mood is better left in the reader's imagination;
- whether the visual would prematurely resolve ambiguity;
- whether a page turn can strengthen the transition;
- whether a comic scene would benefit more from textual timing than pictorial interruption.

### Typography/layout
Slow atmospheric sections can tolerate more breathing room.
High-speed S23-S25 should avoid excessive interruptions or decorative matter.
S17-S19 require careful transition treatment because the book changes genre-pressure there without actually becoming a different story.

### Reader aids
Reader notes should not interrupt S19-S25.
Any glossary/context aids are better outside the main chase sequence.
