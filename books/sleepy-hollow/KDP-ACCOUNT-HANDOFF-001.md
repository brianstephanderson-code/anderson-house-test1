## Product description — KDP public-domain differentiation opening

This illustrated edition includes:

- 12 original illustrations relevant to Irving's story
- Original reader notes on the tale's frame, comedy, ambiguity, and setting
- A source-faithful layout designed around the story's pacing and atmosphere

Washington Irving's classic tale follows the schoolmaster Ichabod Crane through the dreamy Dutch settlements of the Hudson Valley, where appetite, courtship, rivalry, local folklore, and a midnight encounter with the Headless Horseman converge.

This Anderson House edition preserves Irving's public-domain text while adding an original visual and reader-support layer. The illustrations are placed according to the story's literary movement rather than at fixed intervals, leaving the fastest parts of the final chase comparatively uninterrupted.

## Human/account-only completion points

These cannot be truthfully completed by the repository build alone:

1. Sign in to the correct KDP account.
2. Create/edit the Hardcover title.
3. Choose the publishing-rights and marketplace/territory settings.
4. Assign the KDP free ISBN (or deliberately replace this route with an owned ISBN).
5. Upload the final interior PDF and wait until processing completes.
6. Launch Cover Creator only after interior processing, because the hardcover geometry depends on final page count.
7. Insert the final front-cover asset / back-cover copy and let Cover Creator generate the case-laminate cover.
8. Launch KDP Previewer and inspect every reported issue.
9. Set price/marketplaces.
10. Press Publish only after Linda/human preview approval.

## Final stop rule

The Anderson House factory may issue **READY_FOR_KDP_ACCOUNT_UPLOAD** after:
- final interior file passes Linda visual/preflight QA;
- final front-cover asset passes title/quality QA;
- metadata packet matches both;
- current KDP rules have been rechecked.

It may NOT issue **PUBLISHED DONE** until the KDP account-side Previewer and submission steps have actually occurred.
