## BILL — SURVEY RETURNS

### Literary/source constraints
- Every illustration must anchor to a genuine Irving scene or function.
- It must preserve the story's ambiguity, humor, eeriness, sleepy atmosphere, and period setting where applicable.
- It must not rewrite or silently contradict Irving.
- Historical/reference material may inform visual accuracy, but its rights/provenance must be recorded.

### KDP constraints
- KDP requires disclosure of AI-generated images used in a new or republished book.
- KDP does not require disclosure of AI-assisted content.
- AI-generated includes actual images created by an AI tool, even if substantially edited later.
- Publisher remains responsible for intellectual-property compliance and customer experience.
- Print artwork should be prepared at print-suitable quality; Anderson House target is **300 PPI at intended print size** unless a later KDP-specific rule requires otherwise.
- Public-domain differentiation requires the illustrations to be original and relevant, not decorative filler.

### Legal/provenance constraints
For every illustration record:
- production tool/provider
- source/reference material
- source URLs/records where material
- rights/license status
- AI role
- transform history
- output version/hash when practical
- Linda status

## TED — SHARED VISUAL CONTRACT

### Visual purpose
Create an illustrated literary edition that feels:
- autumnal
- sleepy
- eerie
- historically grounded
- occasionally comic
- restrained rather than generic horror

### Protected literary rules
1. Irving's source text is never altered to accommodate an image.
2. Images serve the reading flow; text does not serve the images.
3. Do not depict uncertain supernatural events as objectively proven when Irving leaves room for ambiguity.
4. Character appearance must derive from source description first.
5. Do not introduce modern objects, clothing, architecture, or iconography without a deliberate editorial reason.
6. Do not add invented story events merely to create spectacle.

### Shared style requirements
- coherent style across the edition
- enough tonal range to reproduce well in black-ink hardcover
- strong silhouettes and readable values
- avoid relying on subtle color differences for meaning
- leave composition room for page trim/crop where appropriate
- no embedded text unless specifically required by the brief
- no watermarks, logos, signatures from tools/providers, or third-party marks

### Print target
Prototype trim: **5.5 × 8.5 inches**
Interior: black ink on cream paper candidate
Illustration use: primarily full-page or near-full-page, no bleed by default
Production target: **300 PPI at final placed dimensions**
Final dimensions remain subject to real layout/render testing.

### AI classification
Each illustration must carry exactly one:
- **NONE** — created without AI-based generation or assistance.
- **AI-ASSISTED** — human-created image; AI only used to refine/edit/check/brainstorm.
- **AI-GENERATED** — actual image content created by an AI-based tool, even if later edited.

If AI-GENERATED:
- preserve tool/provider and generation provenance internally;
- flag for KDP disclosure at publication;
- Linda must verify rights and KDP content-policy fit.

### Rights route
Accepted routes:
- original human artwork created for Anderson House;
- AI-generated artwork through a tool whose terms permit the intended commercial use;
- licensed artwork with compatible modification/commercial rights;
- public-domain source/reference material used lawfully;
- independently built artwork informed by functional/historical references.

Rejected without recast:
- unclear ownership;
- copied contemporary artwork;
- watermarked material;
- incompatible license;
- unauthorized celebrity/person likeness or protected brand use;
- provenance too weak to establish a permitted route.
