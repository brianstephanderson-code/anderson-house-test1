## BILL — SURVEY

Bill surveys the protected source itself and identifies moments where an illustration performs a reader function:
- establish place/mood,
- establish character,
- clarify historical setting,
- heighten contrast,
- mark structural transition,
- intensify suspense,
- support memory of a key scene.

Illustrations must not exist merely to increase page count.

### Candidate 01 — Frontispiece: The Hollow
**Function:** Establish the sleepy, enchanted valley before the story properly begins.
**Source anchor:** opening description of the quiet valley, dreamy influence, local apparitions and the Headless Horseman legend.
**Reader effect:** mood / place / expectation.
**Visual elements:** Hudson/Tappan Zee region, secluded valley, autumnal atmosphere, distant rider suggested rather than over-explained.
**Risk:** avoid turning the opening into generic horror; retain dreamy stillness.

### Candidate 02 — Ichabod Crane portrait
**Function:** Fix the comic physical character Irving describes.
**Source anchor:** tall, exceedingly lank schoolmaster, long limbs, huge ears, long nose, weathercock/scarecrow comparison.
**Reader effect:** character recognition + comic tone.
**Risk:** caricature should derive from Irving, not modern cartoon convention alone.

### Candidate 03 — The schoolhouse
**Function:** Establish Ichabod's everyday world before the supernatural arc.
**Source anchor:** one-room log schoolhouse, patched windows, brook, birch tree, pupils' murmur.
**Reader effect:** setting / historical texture / calm before escalation.

### Candidate 04 — Ichabod reading at dusk
**Function:** Show the bridge between reading supernatural tales and projecting them onto the landscape.
**Source anchor:** Ichabod reading Cotton Mather by the brook until dusk, then interpreting ordinary night sounds as ominous.
**Reader effect:** establishes imagination/fear mechanism.
**Risk:** distinguish subjective fear from confirmed supernatural event.

### Candidate 05 — Katrina and the Van Tassel farm
**Function:** Introduce both Katrina and the abundance Ichabod covets.
**Source anchor:** Katrina's introduction and the prosperous Van Tassel farm/barn/orchards.
**Reader effect:** character + motive + comic material desire.

### Candidate 06 — Brom Bones on Daredevil
**Function:** Establish the rival as physical counterweight to Ichabod.
**Source anchor:** Brom's broad frame, fox-tail cap, horsemanship, gang, favorite horse Daredevil.
**Reader effect:** rivalry / energy / threat / humor.

### Candidate 07 — Van Tassel autumn feast
**Function:** Render one of Irving's richest abundance passages.
**Source anchor:** cakes, pies, meats, preserves, milk, cream and steaming teapot at the Dutch country tea-table.
**Reader effect:** sensory immersion + comic appetite.
**Risk:** food details should support, not overwhelm, narrative pace.

### Candidate 08 — Ichabod dancing
**Function:** Capture comic motion and social setting.
**Source anchor:** Ichabod dancing with Katrina while Brom watches in jealousy.
**Reader effect:** comic release + triangle of characters + movement.

### Candidate 09 — Ghost stories after the dance
**Function:** Transition from warmth/sociality into the supernatural night ride.
**Source anchor:** older guests recounting war stories followed by ghosts, apparitions and local superstitions.
**Reader effect:** tonal hinge.
**Visual idea:** warm interior foreground / dark road beyond.

### Candidate 10 — Wiley's Swamp / haunted bridge
**Function:** Begin the final suspense sequence.
**Source anchor:** rough-log bridge, dark oaks/chestnuts/vines, haunted-stream associations, Gunpowder balking.
**Reader effect:** spatial suspense / threshold crossing.

### Candidate 11 — The Headless Horseman revealed
**Function:** Deliver the story's central apparition image.
**Source anchor:** large black horse, towering cloaked rider, revelation that the rider is headless and carries the head at the saddle.
**Reader effect:** climax / recognition.
**Risk:** preserve darkness and ambiguity; do not over-detail.

### Candidate 12 — The chase and thrown head
**Function:** Visual climax and bridge-to-aftermath.
**Source anchor:** chase toward the church bridge, Ichabod looks back, rider rises and throws the head.
**Reader effect:** kinetic climax.
**Risk:** retain the possibility of prank/ambiguity rather than depicting supernatural certainty.

## LINDA — ADMISSION RULE

Linda verifies every illustration individually.

Required checks:
1. **RELEVANT** — clearly tied to the book and scene.
2. **ORIGINAL** — original artwork for this edition, not an unlicensed copied image.
3. **SOURCE-FAITHFUL** — does not materially contradict Irving.
4. **MOOD-FIT** — supports the intended eerie/sleepy/autumnal/comic balance.
5. **RIGHTS-CLEAR** — tool, references, assets and output route are permitted.
6. **AI-CLASSIFIED** — AI-generated vs AI-assisted status recorded where applicable.
7. **PRINT-FIT** — suitable resolution/format for final print use.
8. **NO-FILLER** — earns its place as part of the reader product.

Statuses:
- VERIFIED_FOR_EDITION
- RECAST
- REJECTED
- RIGHTS_HOLD
- TECHNICAL_HOLD

Only **VERIFIED_FOR_EDITION** artwork reaches the hardcover assembler.
