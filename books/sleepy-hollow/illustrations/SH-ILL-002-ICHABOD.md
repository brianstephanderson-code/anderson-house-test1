# SH-ILL-002 — Ichabod Crane Portrait

**SOURCE_ANCHOR:** Irving's physical description of Ichabod Crane  
**FUNCTION / READER PURPOSE:** Establish the comic visual identity of the protagonist from Irving's own description.

## BILL — SURVEY
Source traits:
- tall and exceedingly lank
- narrow shoulders
- long arms and legs
- large ears
- long nose
- scarecrow / weathercock comparisons
- schoolmaster rather than heroic-gothic figure

Historical/reference needs:
- rural schoolmaster clothing of the period
- avoid anachronistic costume shorthand

## TED — BRIEF
**MUST SHOW**
- elongated lank proportions
- expressive but source-grounded face
- period schoolmaster clothing
- comic dignity rather than grotesque mockery

**MUST NOT INVENT / CONTRADICT**
- no muscular/action-hero build
- no Victorian-era costume if period-inaccurate
- no cartoon exaggeration that destroys literary tone

**MOOD:** comic, observant, slightly eccentric  
**COMPOSITION:** portrait or three-quarter standing figure
