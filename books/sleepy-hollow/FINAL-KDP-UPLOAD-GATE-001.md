## Linda — final verification

### Interior
- correct trim
- page count within KDP hardcover range
- source text intact
- no unintended blank/filler pages
- page numbers / front/back matter coherent
- illustration count >= 10 and each is relevant/original
- print quality fit
- no crop marks/comments/watermarks/placeholders
- fonts embedded / PDF readable

### Cover
- exact template matches frozen page count / paper / ink / trim
- title/author match metadata
- safe areas respected
- wrap and hinge respected
- no important content in barcode area
- PDF readable and unprotected

### Rights / legal / provenance
- public-domain status recorded for underlying Irving text
- no Project Gutenberg trademark/license material unintentionally redistributed as part of the paid edition
- all added artwork and edition matter has a permitted commercial-use route
- AI-generated content, if any, classified and disclosed as required
- external-provider terms current enough for submission

### KDP account
- account in good standing
- tax/bank/KYC actions clear
- publishing-rights selection consistent with public-domain edition
- current KDP preview passes before final publish click
