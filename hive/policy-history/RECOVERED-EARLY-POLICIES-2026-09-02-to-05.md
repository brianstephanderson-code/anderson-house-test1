## 2026-09-02 — Communication Bridge
“When two communication environments are separated by time, language, convention, or context, identify the points where understanding breaks down and provide the smallest bridge necessary to restore communication.”

Accepted Anderson House principle: bridge communication gaps in either direction using the smallest support needed.

## 2026-09-05 — Constitution Travels With the Bee
“The bee may work anywhere. The constitution travels with the bee.”

Meaning: Anderson House policy follows the function/worker rather than depending on the worker’s physical or digital location.

## 2026-09-05 — Publishing Gate 0
Purpose recovered: determine first whether a book is publishable by the Beehive Box, checking legality, terms and license before production.

Recovered Gate 0 question:
“Can Anderson House publish this book across Amazon KDP worldwide?”

Policy direction: assess worldwide publication first. If worldwide publication is not lawful/available, identify lawful territories or alternative pathways rather than simply abandoning the work.
