# Anderson House — Recursive Logic / Bridge Engine Policy

Status: ACTIVE CANDIDATE POLICY
Added: 2026-09-19

## Nucleus
STATE -> TRANSFORM -> DONE.

The nucleus stays small. Capability grows through policies governing how a proposed transformation earns DONE.

## Recursive validation
A returned YES or NO is not automatically the end.

For each material claim:
1. Ask: HOW COME?
2. Require evidence/reasoning sufficient for the claim.
3. Ask what follows from that answer.
4. Open new questions/petals only when they could materially change the transformation or DONE.
5. Continue until material branches are verified, rejected, bounded as unknown, or reach diminishing useful returns.
6. Then advance.

Do not recurse forever. Stop when further questions are immaterial to the parent DONE or evidence cannot presently resolve them.

## Symmetric learning
NO -> Why no? -> preserve what worked -> find/repair/redirect the road -> retest.
YES -> Why yes? -> identify the mechanism -> attack the YES -> retest.
Both success and failure produce reusable knowledge.

## Correct the policy too
If repeated problems survive changes to the transformation, question the rule, assumption, gate, STATE representation, or DONE definition itself.
No policy has diplomatic immunity from verification or Kill-the-Riddick testing.

## Triple-loop check
When useful ask:
- Are we doing it right?
- Are we doing the right thing?
- Are we learning/testing it the right way?
The engine's learning method may itself be transformed.

## Real-world state
When humans operate or consume the system, STATE should distinguish:
- work-as-imagined / official description;
- work-as-done / operator practice;
- work-as-experienced / end-user experience.
Reconcile these at the Bridge rather than assuming one is reality.

## Prevention over repeated correction
When a recurring failure is understood, prefer changing the gate/process so the known failure becomes difficult or impossible, rather than repeatedly catching and repairing it.

## Provenance
Material conclusions should retain:
- source/evidence;
- what the evidence actually supports;
- important limits/conditions;
- transformation/decision made from it;
- verification result.
A source is not proof until its meaning and limits are qualified.

## Search-engine application
The Bridge/Search engine seeks the best-supported logical road from known STATE to required DONE.
It may use Shredder, Many Roads, two funnels, end-user evidence, Borging, Aikido, State-izing, independent verification, and Kill the Riddick as modular functions.

It must not force an answer merely because a question was asked. "Unknown with current evidence" is a valid logical result.

## Anti-phishing / security
Research logic must never override security or authorization boundaries.
Do not solicit, expose, transmit, or use passwords, private keys, tokens, recovery codes, or other secrets.
Do not imitate trusted parties or use deceptive collection methods.
A road that requires phishing, credential theft, deception, or unauthorized access is RED and rejected.

## Promotion rule
A useful discovery becomes reusable policy only after it survives appropriate verification. Store the road plus its logic/provenance so later bees can inspect WHY it was accepted.
