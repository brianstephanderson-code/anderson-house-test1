# Anderson House — Two-End Funnel Survey Policy

Status: ACTIVE CANDIDATE POLICY
Added: 2026-09-23

## Purpose
Survey real-world experience without mistaking one source environment for the whole population.

## Two ends
Use two deliberately different source environments when source incentives or selection could shape the answer.

### Narrow end — SHOP DOOR
Collect reports from people closest to the product or service:
- verified buyers;
- retailer reviews;
- direct product users;
- product-specific communities.

This end provides depth and detailed user experience.

### Wide end — CITY CENTER
Collect reports from broader independent environments:
- Reddit;
- independent forums;
- discussion boards;
- complaint threads;
- casual user conversations;
- other places where people may mention the product without being prompted to review it.

This end provides breadth and less-selected experience.

## Counting rule
1. Keep the two ponds separate first.
2. Remove obvious duplicate users/reposts.
3. Count firsthand outcome reports once.
4. Separate actual use reports from people only debating theory.
5. Use simple outcome buckets appropriate to the question. Default buckets:
   - clear positive;
   - delayed or mixed positive;
   - unwanted/worsening;
   - no clear benefit / unsure.
6. Calculate percentages within each pond before combining.
7. If combined, preserve the original pond labels so selection effects remain visible.

## Pattern rule
Do not treat the percentage alone as the product.
Look for the SHAPE that survives across source environments:
- repeated outcomes;
- repeated user language;
- repeated timing;
- repeated failures;
- repeated zero-output cases.

A signal that survives from shop door to city center is more interesting than a signal visible in only one pond.

## Open-door rule
A source-bias caveat is not a stopping point.
Convert it into a testable STATE:
SOURCE MAY BE SELECTED -> FIND A DIFFERENT SOURCE ENVIRONMENT -> RECOUNT -> COMPARE SHAPE.

## Stop rule
Do not widen forever.
Stop when materially different source environments have been sampled and further widening is unlikely to change the useful shape for the parent DONE.

## Shorthand
SHOP DOOR = DEPTH.
CITY CENTER = BREADTH.
COMPARE SHAPE = BRIDGE.
